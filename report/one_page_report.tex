\sect{2. Proposed improvement 1: an update-resistance protocol}
\begin{itemize}
\item Inject a counterfactual: in the gold passage, swap the answer object $o$ for a plausible false $o'$. Grid: $n \in \{1,3\}$ stale copies of the old passage $\times$ $k \in \{0,1,2,3,5\}$ corrections. Dense retrieval on the same index is the control.
\item Per-stage attribution of every miss: A not among the candidate facts, B removed by the filter, C not seeded, D old passage scores higher, E outside the top 5.
\item Blind: the grid's result files were sealed by sha256 before analysis, and the prediction was committed (\texttt{460c50a}) before any result was opened.
\item N = 75 swaps (one per gold passage), kept from 120 after two screening passes by Claude with written reasons; I own the screening rule. % RB header, RB section 6
\end{itemize}

\sect{3. Proposed improvement 2: contradiction-aware seeding}
Among the facts kept by the filter, if two share subject and relation but differ in object, drop the older one before seeding PageRank
(\texttt{drop\_superseded}, \texttt{experiments/extension/attrib\_rag.py}). Detection is an oracle: the correction copies the old triple with only the object changed.

\sect{5. Findings}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The filter is not the bottleneck; the last-hop fact rarely becomes a seed at all.} The corrected fact is among the 5 candidate facts in 9 of 75 questions, and the filter keeps 8 of those 9. % P
Before any correction the old fact is also a candidate in only 9 of 75, so most passages reach the top 5 through their own passage seed and graph spread. % U section 2
\item \textbf{Stale copies hurt the graph, not dense} ($-11.7$ vs $-0.7$ points). In the 35 records lost at $n=3$, an old copy is in the top 5 in 19, and the old-versus-new PageRank order follows passage cosine (same sign in 34 of 35, $\rho = +0.91$), not node degree ($\rho = +0.14$). % RB section 3 / U section 3
\item \textbf{The fix is correct but rarely has a seed to act on}: it fires in 7 of 75 questions, and those already succeed. % P
\end{itemize}

\sect{6. Next proposed improvements (proposals, not implemented)}
\begin{itemize}
\item A larger \texttt{linking\_top\_k}, or linking aimed at the last hop, so the corrected fact can become a seed.
\item Resolve contradictions at write time with validity intervals (as Zep/Graphiti do), or collapse duplicate stale passages at indexing.
\item Put dates on passages so the reader can prefer the newer value.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Limits.} Synthetic swaps; oracle contradiction detection; N = 75; one dataset (MuSiQue); one LLM (gpt-4o-mini).
